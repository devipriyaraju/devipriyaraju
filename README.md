# Hi, I am Devipriya Raju 👩‍💻

I am a **Machine Learning Researcher, Computer Vision Engineer, and Data Scientist** based in Edmonton, Canada.

My recent work focuses on **3D human motion, pose estimation, and physics-aware learning**, alongside **medical imaging, wearable-sensor modeling, and applied data science**. I care about building reliable AI systems and evaluating them honestly, so that reported results hold up on people and data the model has never seen.

## 🔭 What I Work On

* 3D human motion modeling, articulated tracking, and human dynamics
* Human and infant pose estimation
* Semantic segmentation and 3D scene understanding (NeRF)
* Medical imaging and clinical AI
* Wearable and physiological signal modeling
* Statistical modeling, analytics, and business intelligence
* Multimodal and agentic AI applications

## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🏃 3D Human Motion and Physics-Aware Learning

| Project | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Occlusion-Robust Foot-to-Ground Contact](https://github.com/devipriyaraju/Occlusion-Robust-Foot-to-Ground-Contact-from-3D-Human-Motion-) | Foot contact prediction under occlusion with SMPL-H, ground reaction force estimation, inverse dynamics, and contact-driven motion correction |
| [Human Dynamics From Motion](https://github.com/devipriyaraju/Human-Dynamics-From-Motion) | Predicts ground reaction forces and lower-limb joint torques from 3D motion using temporal convolutional networks and physics-aware learning |

| [Physics-Aware Human Dynamics](https://github.com/devipriyaraju/Physics-Aware-Human-Dynamics) | Newton-Euler inverse dynamics, ground reaction force estimation, joint torque analysis, and hybrid temporal learning |
